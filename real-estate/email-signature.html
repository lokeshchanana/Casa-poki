🚀 How to Copy & Paste into Gmail (15 Seconds):

  1. Choose the signature design you prefer below and click “📋 Copy to Clipboard” (or highlight the signature box and press Ctrl+C / Cmd+C).
  2. Open Gmail Settings → click “See all settings” → stay on the “General” tab.
  3. Scroll down to the “Signature” section, click “+ Create new” (e.g., name it Loki - Luxury RE).
  4. Click inside the signature editor and paste with Ctrl+V (Windows) or Cmd+V (Mac).
  5. Under “Signature defaults”, select your new signature for both FOR NEW EMAILS USE and ON REPLY/FORWARD USE.
  6. Scroll to the very bottom and click “Save Changes”. Done!
