🚀 How to apply to your Gmail in 15 seconds:

  1. Choose your preferred design below and click the “📋 Copy to Clipboard” button (or highlight the signature with your mouse and press Ctrl+C / Cmd+C).
  2. Open Gmail Settings → click “See all settings” → stay on the “General” tab.
  3. Scroll down to the “Signature” section, click “+ Create new” (e.g. named Loki - Real Estate).
  4. Click inside the signature text box and paste with Ctrl+V (Windows) or Cmd+V (Mac).
  5. Under “Signature defaults”, select your new signature for FOR NEW EMAILS USE and ON REPLY/FORWARD USE.
  6. Scroll to the very bottom and click “Save Changes”. Done!
