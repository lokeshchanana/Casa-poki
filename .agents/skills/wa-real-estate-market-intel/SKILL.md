# Washington Real Estate Market Intel & Viral Format Skill

## 1. Geographic Focus
- **Core Counties:** King (Seattle, Bellevue, Kirkland, Redmond, Renton, Kent, Auburn, Shoreline, Bothell, Burien), Pierce (Tacoma, Puyallup, Fife), Snohomish (Everett, Lynnwood, Edmonds, Woodinville), Thurston (Olympia).
- **Price Sweet Spot:** `$550,000 – $1,100,000` everyday residential homes, townhomes, and new construction.

## 2. Market Update Research Rubric
Every WA Market Update brief must include:
1. **Hard Numbers (with source & date):** Median sale price YoY/MoM, active inventory/months of supply, median days on market, and current 30-year fixed mortgage rate context (e.g., Freddie Mac PMMS / NWMLS monthly report).
2. **Hyper-Local Angle:** Why it matters for a specific Puget Sound pocket (e.g., Link Light Rail expansion in South King / Shoreline, Eastside school district demand, Pierce County affordability gap).
3. **Actionable Takeaway:** One clear, protective takeaway for buyers and one for sellers—zero fearmongering.

## 3. Viral Format & Hook Decomposition
When scouting popular formats on Instagram Reels, TikTok, or X:
- Extract the **structure**, never copy someone else's script or footage:
  - **0–2s Hook:** Visual pattern interrupt + specific local curiosity gap (e.g., *"What $750k actually buys in Kent vs. Bothell right now"*).
  - **2–15s Value Beat:** 2–3 fast visual cuts or numbered cards with concrete numbers.
  - **15–35s Payoff:** Surprising local nuance or protective buyer/seller tip.
  - **Last 3s CTA:** Low-pressure invitation (*"DM me 'MAP' or text (801) 860-1052 for the full breakdown"*).
