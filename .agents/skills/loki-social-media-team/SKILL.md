# Lokesh Chanana — Social Media Team Runbook

Always read [product-marketing.md](../../product-marketing.md), [loki-brand-design](../loki-brand-design/SKILL.md), and [loki-avatar-studio](../loki-avatar-studio/SKILL.md) before producing any social asset.

## 1. Active Content Mode: STATIC IMAGES ONLY (ZERO AI SOUND)
- **NO AI VOICE / TTS:** Do NOT generate AI-voiced Reels or TikToks (`edge-tts` / synthetic voices are disabled). Voiceover video formats stay paused until Lokesh provides his personal voice model.
- Focus 100% on crisp, high-save **static graphics & carousels** combining Lokesh's **approved 2D flat-vector avatars** (`/tank/social/avatars/`) and **curated real photos** (`/tank/social/photos/`).

## 3. Strict Human-in-the-Loop Approval Gate (CRITICAL)
- **NEVER auto-publish** to any platform.
- Every post lands in `PENDING_APPROVAL` in `Social Studio` (`checkin.lokeshchanana.com/?tab=social`) and only dispatches to **Postiz** after Lokesh clicks **Approve & Schedule**.

## 4. Mandatory Editorial & Legal Checklist
- **Brokerage Prominence (WA RCW 18.85):** Include `Lokesh Chanana (Loki) • Real Broker LLC`.
- **Listing Credit:** Always credit `Listed by [Agent] | [Brokerage]` when highlighting another agent's listing.
- **Fair Housing:** Zero steering or demographic-targeting language.
- **Zero AI Tells:** No em dashes (`—`), no *"It's not X, it's Y"*, no *"No X, no Y, no Z"*, no *"Agree?"* / *"Let that sink in."*, no emoji-bullet spam.
